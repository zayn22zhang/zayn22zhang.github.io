My research interests include quantum information theory, quantum foundations and quantum cryptography.

In quantum foundations, I am interested in Bell nonlocality and the role of quantum resource Theory as a unified framework for characterizing and quantifying physical scenario. Also quantum gravity is an appealing topic.

In quantum information theory, I am particularly interested in some mathematical and information-theoretic tools to explore quantum systems. 

I am also interested in multipartite entanglement and its application to quantum networks or quantum key distribution. Also I want to do something about combine Bell nonlocality and DIQKD.
