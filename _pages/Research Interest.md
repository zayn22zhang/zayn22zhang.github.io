My research focuses on the mathematical and information-theoretic characterization of quantum entanglement and correlations, particularly multipartite systems and their applications to quantum networks and quantum key distribution (QKD). In quantum foundations, I investigate Bell nonlocality, quantum resource theories as unified quantitative frameworks, quantum reference frames at the intersection with quantum gravity, and generalized probabilistic theories (GPTs) to investigate the fundamental structure of quantum mechanics.
